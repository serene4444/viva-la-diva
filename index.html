How to get your export
  1. Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Pick your account, then "Some of your information" → Followers and following.
  3. Choose "Download to device", format JSON, date range "All time".
  4. Instagram emails you a link, usually within minutes. Download the .zip and drop it here.

    FAQ

    Do you need my Instagram password?

    Never. You download your own data from Instagram and open it here. The file is read inside this page and never uploaded anywhere.

    Why do I have to download an export?

    It's the only way to see your lists without logging in through a third party, which can get your account flagged. The export is Instagram's own official copy of your data.

    What do the labels mean?

    They're based on how long you've followed someone who doesn't follow you back. Situationship: under 3 months, so they might still come around. Fan girl: 3 months to a year. Delulu: 1 to 3 years. Certified delulu: 3 years or more. On your other lists, Secret admirer follows you but you don't follow back, and Mutual queen means you follow each other.

    Does it unfollow people for me?

    No. Tap their name to open their profile and unfollow them there, then tap Cut off here to tick them off and watch your list shrink.

    Someone on my list actually follows me. Why?

    Your export is a snapshot from the day you downloaded it. If they followed you after that, download a fresh export.

    Why aren't celebrities on my list?

    Celebrities, athletes, and big brands don't follow fans back, so they're filtered out automatically and kept in the Celebs tab. It only matches their exact official usernames, so fan pages and fake accounts still show up. If a celeb slips through, tap Hide. If someone got filtered by mistake, tap Not a celeb in the Celebs tab.

    Why are there no profile pictures?

    Instagram's export only includes usernames and dates, so each person gets a monogram instead. Tap the name to see their real profile.
