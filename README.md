# Viva la Diva

> You were never meant to be anyone's fan girl. Take back control of who your real friends are.

A pink, chic web page that shows who you follow on Instagram that doesn't follow you back.

## How it works

1. In Instagram, go to **Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information**.
2. Choose **Followers and following**, format **JSON**, date range **All time**.
3. Open the site and drop in the `.zip` Instagram emails you.

Everything runs in your browser. No login, no password, and your data never leaves your device.

## Features

- Labels for everyone who doesn't follow back: Situationship, Fan girl, Delulu, Certified delulu
- Secret admirers, mutual queens, and a hidden list
- A "cut them off" checklist with a progress bar
- Animated art: blinking eyes that roll at your results, a cat you can pet, and a cat that walks across the page

Built as a single `index.html` and hosted on GitHub Pages.
